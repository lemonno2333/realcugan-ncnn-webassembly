Free online Real-CUGAN anime image upscaler

Real-CUGAN enlarges anime art, illustrations, and line art at 2×, 3×, or 4× in your browser. It restores lines, flat-color detail, and compression damage locally through WebAssembly; images are never uploaded.

Choosing between the SE and Pro models

SE supports 2×, 3×, and 4×, so choose it whenever you need 4×. Pro supports 2× and 3× and is a good first choice when you want more natural-looking lines and flat-color transitions.

What the denoise levels actually do

Use none for clean originals, conservative when you want to preserve style and texture, and denoise 1/2 for light compression on SE at 2×. Use denoise 3 only for obvious noise or block artifacts; stronger denoise can flatten fine detail.
